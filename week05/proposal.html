Website Plan: Hostel Management System

A digital solution for modern student living.

Site Name

Proposed Name: PickFord Acres Hostel

Reason: This name combines a sense of choice ("Pick") with the expansive, comfortable environment implied by "Acres." It positions the hostel as a premium, spacious destination for students and room seekers.

Optional Domain: pickford-hostel.com

Site Purpose

The primary goal is to transition from manual, error-prone booking methods to an automated digital solution[cite: 5, 6]. The site serves as a bridge for room seekers to view "Golden Rooms" and secure accommodations through a streamlined online form.

Scenarios

  • "I am a new student looking for a premium room; where can I see the best available options and their photos?"
  • "I need to book a room immediately; is there a form I can fill out to provide my details and secure a stay?"

Color Scheme

  • Primary (#2C3E50): Used for all major headings, navigation bars, and footer backgrounds to convey professionalism.
  • Accent (#E67E22): Used for Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons, links, and decorative borders to draw user attention to booking actions.
  • #F4F7F6 (Light Grey) : For Background.

Typography

  • Georgia (Serif): Used for headers (H1, H2, H3) to provide a sophisticated, "established" feel for the hostel's brand.
  • Segoe UI / Sans-Serif: Used for all body text and form labels to ensure high readability and a modern aesthetic on digital screens.
